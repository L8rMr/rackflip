Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026
The short version RackFlip doesn't collect, sell or share any data about you. It has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It only talks to the Komga servers you add yourself.

What RackFlip stores, and where

  • Your server logins (server address, email and password) are stored in the iPhone or iPad's Keychain, on your device. They are sent only to the server they belong to, so it can sign you in.
  • Downloaded and imported issues are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Removing the app removes them.
  • Your settings (theme, reading mode, hidden magazines and your own categories) are stored on your device.

What leaves your device

Only requests to the Komga servers you add: to list your libraries, load covers and pages, and save your reading position back to that server. Those servers are run by you or by whoever you got the address from, and their own privacy terms apply. The developer of RackFlip doesn't receive any of it.

What RackFlip doesn't do

  • No accounts with us. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Files you import

When you import a CBZ, ZIP or PDF from the Files app, RackFlip copies it into its own storage and reads it on your device. Imported files are never uploaded anywhere.

Children

RackFlip isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/L8rMr/rackflip/issues.
